## 1. High risk of loss

Contracts for difference (CFDs) and leveraged foreign exchange are complex products. Their prices can move quickly and you can lose your entire deposit in a short time. These products are not suitable for everyone. Never trade with money you need for essential living costs or cannot afford to lose.

## 2. Leverage magnifies gains and losses

Leverage lets you open a position worth much more than the margin you deposit. A small price movement against you produces a loss proportional to the full position, not to your margin. Higher leverage means losses can exceed what you expected very quickly.

## 3. You do not own the underlying asset

CFDs give you exposure to the price of an instrument such as a currency pair, index, commodity or cryptocurrency. You do not acquire ownership, voting rights or delivery of the underlying asset.

## 4. Margin calls and stop-outs

You must keep enough equity to support your open positions. If your margin level falls to the stop-out level of your account, positions may be closed automatically at the prevailing price, which may be worse than you expected.

## 5. Volatility, gaps and slippage

Prices can jump between levels without trading in between, for example at the market open, around economic releases or during unexpected events. Stop-loss orders are executed at the next available price and do not guarantee a particular exit price. Orders may be filled at a better or worse price than requested (slippage).

## 6. Liquidity and market conditions

In thin or disorderly markets, spreads can widen significantly and it may be difficult or impossible to open or close positions at the prices you want.

## 7. Cryptocurrency CFDs

Cryptocurrency prices are especially volatile and can be affected by regulatory announcements, technical failures, network events and sentiment. Markets for some coins can be thin. Treat crypto positions as particularly high risk.

## 8. Overnight costs and currency risk

Positions held past the daily rollover may be charged or credited a swap. If your account currency differs from the currency of an instrument, exchange rate movements also affect your results.

## 9. Technology risk

Trading relies on internet connections, devices and platforms that can fail or be delayed. You are responsible for your own equipment and connection. We are not liable for losses caused by failures outside our reasonable control.

## 10. Negative balance

In extreme conditions, losses may exceed the funds in your account. How a negative balance is treated is set out in the Client Agreement.
