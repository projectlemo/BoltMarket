## 3. Execution factors

When executing orders, the Company takes all sufficient steps to obtain the best possible result, considering:

- **price**, the most important factor for most orders;
- **costs**, including spread and commission;
- **speed** of execution;
- **likelihood of execution and settlement**;
- **size and type** of the order;
- **market conditions** at the time, including volatility and liquidity.

## 4. Pricing

The Company's prices are derived from feeds of the underlying markets provided by third-party sources. Bid and ask prices are shown in the trading platform. Prices on the website are indicative only.

## 5. Order types

- **Market orders** are executed at the next available price.
- **Limit orders** (buy limit, sell limit) are executed at the specified price or better.
- **Stop orders** (buy stop, sell stop) become market orders when the specified price is reached and are executed at the next available price.
- **Stop-limit orders** place a limit order when the stop price is reached.
- **Stop-loss and take-profit** orders close a position at the specified level or the next available price.

## 6. Slippage

In fast or illiquid markets, the price at which an order is executed may differ from the price requested. Slippage can be positive or negative and is applied in the same way in both directions.

## 7. Gaps and market opens

If the market opens or moves beyond the level of a pending or stop order, the order is executed at the first available price, which may be significantly different from the order level. This can happen after weekends, holidays and major events.

## 8. Spreads and trading hours

Spreads are variable and can widen around news, at the daily rollover and when liquidity is low. Each instrument trades during the sessions shown in its specification in MetaTrader 5. Orders cannot be executed outside trading sessions.

## 9. Execution venues and technology

Orders are received and processed through the MetaTrader 5 trading server. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by your device, internet connection or third-party networks.
