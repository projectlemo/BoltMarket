MetaTrader 5 can look busy the first time you open it. This guide takes you through a complete first session: logging in, finding a market, reading the chart, placing an order with protection attached and closing it. Use a [demo account](/trading/demo-account) while you practise.

## 1. Log in

1. Open an MT5 account in the Bolt Markets client portal. Note the **login number**, **password** and **server** shown for that account.
2. Install MetaTrader 5 from the [platform page](/trading/metatrader-5) or open the web platform.
3. Choose **File → Login to Trade Account**, enter the login and password, pick the server and confirm.

A connection indicator in the bottom corner shows when you are online. If it stays red, check that you selected the exact server shown in the portal.

## 2. Find the instruments you want

The **Market Watch** window lists symbols with their bid and ask prices. Right-click it and choose **Symbols** to show more, or simply type a symbol name such as `XAUUSD` into the search field.

Right-click any symbol and choose **Specification** to see its contract size, minimum volume, trading sessions and swap rates. It is worth doing this once for every instrument you trade.

## 3. Open and read a chart

Drag a symbol from Market Watch onto the chart area. Use the toolbar to switch timeframes, from one minute (M1) to monthly (MN). Candlesticks show the open, high, low and close of each period: green or hollow candles closed higher, red or filled candles closed lower.

Add an indicator through **Insert → Indicators**. Start with something simple, such as a moving average, rather than filling the chart.

## 4. Place an order with a stop-loss

Press **F9** or double-click the symbol to open the order window.

- **Volume:** your position size in lots. Work it out first with the [lot size calculator](/tools/lot-size-calculator).
- **Stop Loss:** the price at which the trade closes to cap your loss.
- **Take Profit:** the price at which the trade closes to lock in gains.
- **Type:** *Market Execution* fills now at the current price. A *Pending Order* (limit or stop) waits for a price you choose.

Click **Buy** or **Sell**. Your position appears in the **Trade** tab of the Toolbox at the bottom of the screen, with live profit or loss.

## 5. Manage the position

Double-click the position in the Trade tab to modify the stop or target. You can also drag the stop and target lines directly on the chart. Many traders move the stop to the entry price once the trade has moved a set distance in their favour, which removes the original risk.

## 6. Close the trade

Click the small cross next to the position in the Trade tab, or open the order window and choose **Close**. The result moves to the **History** tab, together with any commission and swap charges.

## Good habits from day one

- Check the margin level in the Trade tab before adding positions.
- Keep the [economic calendar](/tools/economic-calendar) open during the trading day.
- Write down why you took each trade. A short journal will teach you more than any indicator.

Once these steps feel routine on demo, you are ready to repeat them on a live account with a small size.
